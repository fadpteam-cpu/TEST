build-over agreements in London

Thames Water build-over and build-near agreements — coordination, drawings, structural detail.

What a build-over agreement is

If your proposed extension passes over, under, or within three metres of a public sewer, you need a build-over agreement from Thames Water. We coordinate the entire process — sewer location, structural foundation design to avoid the sewer, application drawings, and Thames Water liaison.

The most common mistake is discovering the sewer after construction has started. We identify the sewer position at feasibility stage, before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a build-over agreement cost? +
Our coordination fee is £750-£2,500. Thames Water application fee: £300-£400. CCTV survey if required: £300-£500.
How long does it take? +
Thames Water targets 21 working days. In practice, 4-8 weeks including preparation.

Fixed fees in writing. First consultation free.
